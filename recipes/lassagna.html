Lasagna Recipe

This is a simple lasagna recipe that serves 4 people.

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 1 package lasagna noodles
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese and half of the mozzarella cheese.
  6. Layer the ingredients in a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes or until the top is golden brown.
