Pizza Recipe

This is a simple pizza recipe that serves 4 people.

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup chopped pepperoni
  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Spread the pizza sauce over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Top with the pepperoni slices.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
