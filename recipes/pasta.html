Pasta Recipe

This is a simple pasta recipe that serves 4 people.

  • 1 pound pasta
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil and sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the fresh basil and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Drain the cooked pasta and toss it with the sauce.
